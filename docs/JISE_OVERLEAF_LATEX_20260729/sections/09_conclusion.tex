\section{CONCLUSION}

This work presents Evidence-Controlled Few-Shot Information Extraction
(EC-FSIE) for low-label Chinese equipment-domain NER. Its A/B/C/D chain isolates
kNN demonstrations, glossary injection, and RAG evidence under a common
audit-preserving execution contract. On the frozen v1 dataset at $K=5$ over
three seeds, kNN and the glossary increase observed F1/recall. C and D remain
near-tied in F1, while D achieves higher precision and 55.0 fewer
false-positive entities on average. RAG therefore yields a precision-oriented
operating-point shift with near-tied F1.

The same interfaces were evaluated on all 1,343 labeled rows of the official
CLUENER development split with separately frozen, schema-specific resources.
The kNN, lexicon, and RAG directions recur descriptively on this additional
Chinese NER benchmark, without implying official-test performance, resource
transfer, or broad cross-domain generalization.

These conclusions are scoped to the evaluated NER setting. Non-test human
calibration reaches overall F1 of 0.8000 but retains weak
\entitytype{TECH\_SCHEME} agreement, and no frozen-test formal IAA is available.
The F7 seed-42 Stage-R audit is reproducible and auditable, yet exact RE F1 is
low, D is below C, and every configuration has zero
\texttt{HAS\_METRIC} true positives. The frozen inventory is mechanically 16/60
complete---four domain Stage-N-plus-Stage-R cells and 12 CLUENER NER cells---and
the other 44 identities remain route-closed. EC-FSIE contributes a reproducible
evidence-control framework that connects bounded evidence channels to
interpretable NER operating points while retaining negative trade-offs as
explicit boundaries for subsequent information-extraction extensions.
