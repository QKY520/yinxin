\section{CONCLUSION}

This work presents a reproducible local-LLM pipeline for low-label Chinese
equipment-domain NER and evaluates kNN demonstrations, glossary injection, and
RAG evidence through frozen-test A/B/C/D ablations over three seeds. In the
current v1 dataset and $K=5$ setting, kNN and the glossary improve F1/recall.
Configurations C and D are near-tied in F1: C is marginally higher in recall
and F1, whereas D has higher precision and 55.0 fewer false-positive entity
predictions on average. The available evidence therefore supports RAG as a
precision-oriented component, not as the best-F1 module or a universal
hallucination-removal method.

The same A/B/C/D directions were also evaluated on all 1,343 labeled rows of
the official CLUENER development split at $K=5$ over three seeds. kNN and the
support-derived schema-specific lexicon show directional NER improvements, and
RAG again preserves near-tied F1 while increasing precision and reducing
false-positive entities. This is limited descriptive portability to one
additional Chinese NER benchmark; it is not broad public-benchmark or
cross-domain generalization.

This conclusion applies only to the frozen Chinese equipment-domain NER
setting studied here. The fresh non-test human calibration has overall F1 of
0.8000 but weak \entitytype{TECH\_SCHEME} agreement, and no frozen-test formal
IAA is available. The separate F7 seed-42 Stage-R audit is retained as an
exploratory negative result: exact RE F1 is low in all four configurations,
D is below C, and all four have zero \texttt{HAS\_METRIC} true positives. It
demonstrates an auditable two-stage execution path but does not support a
positive RE or RAG-for-RE claim. Broad public-benchmark generalization,
$K$-scan robustness, statistical significance, and a complete ablation matrix
remain unverified. The frozen inventory is mechanically 16/60 complete (four
domain Stage-N-plus-Stage-R cells and 12 CLUENER NER cells); the other 44
identities remain route-closed rather than being counted as failed experiments.
The paper's contribution is an evidence-bounded engineering
baseline that preserves both gains and negative trade-offs rather than claiming
a broader completed information-extraction solution.
