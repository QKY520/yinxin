\section{CONCLUSION}

EC-FSIE makes inference-time evidence a controlled interface for low-label
Chinese equipment NER. Its A/B/C/D sequence isolates demonstrations, schema
terminology, and retrieved passages under one traceable execution contract.
On the frozen domain test set, kNN and glossary injection improve observed F1
and recall. The comparison between C and D identifies two deployment-relevant
settings: C favors recall, while D preserves near-tied F1, improves precision,
and reduces mean false-positive entities by 55.0.

The same qualitative sequence recurs on all 1,343 labeled CLUENER development
rows with independently frozen resources. Together, these results support a
reproducible evidence-control approach and an operating-point interpretation of
few-shot generative NER. The current evidence is deliberately scoped to NER;
broader generalization, formal uncertainty analysis, and effective relation
extraction remain targets for subsequent validation.
