\section{DISCUSSION}

The results establish a clear, conditional operating principle for the EC-FSIE
framework. Under frozen v1 labels,
$K=5$, and the current local-model configuration, kNN demonstrations and
glossary injection improve F1/recall, while RAG yields near-tied F1 relative to
C with higher precision and fewer false-positive entities. The practical value
of RAG in this result is not maximizing F1; it is reducing the number of
erroneous candidates that could enter manual review or later knowledge-base
cleaning.

The focused CLUENER block strengthens this interpretation with one additional
Chinese NER benchmark. On all 1,343 labeled official development rows, B--A,
C--B, and D--C follow the same qualitative directions as the domain block:
kNN improves F1, the support-derived schema-specific lexicon gives a modest
directional F1/recall increase, and RAG keeps F1 near tied while increasing
precision and reducing false positives. The public result provides a focused
descriptive replication under independently frozen CLUENER resources. It
strengthens the framework's portability evidence to one additional Chinese NER
schema, while broader public-benchmark transfer and statistical significance
remain outside this evidence line.

The modules have different effects. kNN reduces dependence on a fixed randomly
selected demonstration set. The glossary broadens domain coverage but may also
invite additional false positives. RAG moves the system to a more conservative
precision-oriented point. These effects are not monotonically additive, and
D's lower F1 than C must be retained as a real trade-off rather than hidden by
reporting only the best value.

The calibration audit further narrows interpretation. Overall non-test
calibration F1 of 0.8000 indicates that the locked guideline reached the
original minimum on a small fresh sample, but \entitytype{TECH\_SCHEME}
agreement of 0.153846 shows that this class remains unstable. Aggregate
micro-F1 on the frozen v1 labels may therefore obscure class-specific label
ambiguity. The four entity types should not be treated as equally reliable in
future applications.

The framework's primary evidence line is NER. The final A/B/C/D package is
NER-only, while a separate F7 Stage-R audit supplies four
single-seed, exact-triple measurements: RE F1 is 0.040541, 0.056972, 0.057971,
and 0.045381 for A--D, with zero \texttt{HAS\_METRIC} true positives in every
cell and D below C. This result defines the current RE boundary while also
providing auxiliary reproducibility/auditability value for the framework.
CoVe/self-verification has no positive final-test evidence. Broad
public-benchmark generalization, robustness across $K$ values, and statistical
significance are likewise outside the completed evidence.
