\section{DISCUSSION}

The evaluated EC-FSIE chain reveals a conditional operating principle under
frozen v1 labels, $K=5$, and the current local-model configuration. kNN
demonstrations and glossary injection increase observed F1/recall, whereas RAG
moves the extractor toward higher precision and fewer false-positive entities
with near-tied F1 relative to C. The focused CLUENER block follows the same
directions on all 1,343 labeled official development rows under separately
frozen resources. This recurrence is descriptive and bounded to one additional
Chinese NER benchmark.

The modules are therefore complementary rather than monotonically additive.
kNN controls which labeled evidence conditions the query; the glossary expands
schema-specific coverage and can also invite extra predictions; and RAG changes
the \mbox{precision--recall} operating point. EC-FSIE exposes each transition
while the shared trace layer preserves both improvements and regressions as
reproducible outcomes. Table~\ref{tab:claim-evidence-map} consolidates the
corresponding claim boundaries.

The calibration result conditions the domain interpretation. Aggregate
micro-F1 can obscure the instability of \entitytype{TECH\_SCHEME}, so future
applications should not treat the four entity types as equally reliable. The
relation evidence imposes a separate boundary: Stage R is auditable, but its
low exact-triple scores and zero \texttt{HAS\_METRIC} true positives bound
positive RE effectiveness. CoVe, broad public-benchmark generalization,
robustness across $K$, and statistical significance remain outside the current
evidence.
