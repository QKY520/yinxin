\section{DATASET AND TASK DEFINITION}

\subsection{Construction, Split, and Freeze}

We collected 400 candidate passages from public sources: 7 encyclopedia
passages, 84 patent abstracts, and 309 passages originating from military- or
equipment-related public text associated with CMNEE source material
\cite{zhu2024cmnee}. A project-recorded QAonMilitaryKG seed dictionary was used
only for exact string-match suggestion generation.
\modelname{Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct-AWQ} was also used in the historical proposal
workflow to generate review suggestions; neither machine suggestion nor model
output was accepted as gold by itself.

After human review and structural validation, 250 passages were frozen as
dataset version v1: 30 support passages, 50 development passages, and 170 test
passages. The support split is the sole candidate pool for both fixed-random and
kNN demonstrations. Development data was used before the final test evaluation.
The frozen test set was used only to evaluate final configurations; it was not
used to tune prompts, thresholds, schemas, glossary entries, or retrieval
settings.
The remaining 150 collected candidates were not promoted into the frozen v1
split; they remain collection-stage records rather than evaluation examples.
These fixed roles constitute EC-FSIE's first evidence-control boundary:
demonstration evidence is support-only, development decisions precede the
frozen evaluation, and test labels remain confined to scoring.

The RAG corpus consists of public encyclopedia passages, patent abstracts, and
glossary entries. It was deduplicated against frozen test texts with a fixed
8-gram overlap protocol. Thus, test passages do not enter the demonstration
pool or RAG corpus, and the system does not retrieve the held-out test passage
itself as evidence.

Freezing the split does not mean that all 170 test passages received mutually
blind independent double annotation and formal adjudication. Such a complete
frozen-test annotation chain is unavailable. We therefore do not describe v1
as fully independently double-annotated human gold, and we do not report a
frozen-test formal IAA value.

\subsection{Focused CLUENER Directional-Replication Protocol}

We use CLUENER \cite{xu2020cluener} as one additional Chinese NER benchmark.
Because the official CLUENER test file is distributed without gold labels, it
was not used for offline evaluation. Instead, the complete labeled official
development split---all 1,343 rows---was frozen as the focused final
evaluation set. This is explicitly a limited descriptive labeled-dev
directional replication rather than an official-test leaderboard result or a
broad generalization test.

The CLUENER resources were constructed independently from the equipment-domain
resources. A frozen 30-record CLUENER support bank supplies demonstrations. A
61-entry schema-specific lexicon was derived from that support bank only; it is
not the equipment glossary. The frozen labels-stripped CLUENER train-text RAG
pool contains 9,584 rows, and its construction log records 89 removals under
the fixed 8-gram/0.80 containment blocklist. Offline retrieval produced a
locked top-3 replay for every evaluation query, totaling 4,029 selected
passages across 1,343 rows. Gold development labels entered only the scorer,
never prompts, the support bank, the lexicon, or RAG evidence. This separation
implements the same EC-FSIE evidence-control contract without transferring the
equipment-domain glossary, support examples, or evidence corpus into CLUENER.

The 1,343-row labeled-dev evaluation contains 3,072 gold entity mentions under
the native ten-class CLUENER schema. Table~\ref{tab:cluener-types} records the
class distribution used by every public cell.

\subsection{Entity Types and Metrics}

Table~\ref{tab:entity-types} defines the four equipment-domain entity types;
CLUENER is evaluated under its native published schema rather than remapped to
these four categories.

\begin{table}[ht]

\centering
\begin{tabular}{>{\ttfamily}p{2.3cm}p{8.2cm}}
\toprule
\normalfont Entity type & Definition \\
\midrule
EQUIPMENT & An equipment item, weapon system, platform, or model name. \\
MODULE & A component, module, subsystem, or constituent unit. \\
TECH\_SCHEME & A technical scheme, system, method, or mechanism. \\
METRIC & A performance metric, parameter, or measurement expression. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

An entity prediction is counted as a true positive only when both its span
boundary and type exactly match the reference annotation. We report
entity-level micro precision, recall, and F1, together with the false-positive
entity count. The latter is a count of incorrect predicted entities. Dividing
it by all predicted entities would produce an entity-level
false-discovery-style measure; it is not an open-domain factuality score and is
not treated as a general hallucination metric in this paper.

\subsection{Fresh Two-Human Non-Test Calibration}

To independently assess the operability of the locked annotation guideline,
two human annotators independently labeled 20 previously unused non-test
passages. The passages were not part of the frozen test set. Annotators began
from blank sheets, received distinct randomized orders and blind identifiers,
and were compared without modifying, sorting, completing, or adjudicating
either annotation file. All 20 pairs passed structural checks; there were zero
structural hard errors and zero blocking review items, and both annotators used
all four entity types.
Table~\ref{tab:calibration} reports the resulting exact span-plus-type
agreement by entity type.

Annotators A and B produced 175 and 220 entities, respectively, with 158 exact
span-plus-type matches. Treating A as the reference for the directional
precision/recall report gives precision 0.718182, recall 0.902857, and exact
micro-F1 0.800000; exchanging the reference changes directional precision and
recall but not F1. Five of 20 passages were exactly identical, while 15
retained disagreements.

The overall F1 of 0.8000 exactly met the study's original minimum threshold of
at least 0.80 and was accepted for progression to manuscript preparation. It
has no safety margin and did not reach the later, more conservative target of
at least 0.85. Most importantly, \entitytype{TECH\_SCHEME} F1 is only 0.153846,
based on 6 and 7 annotations from the two annotators. This reveals substantial
class ambiguity between technical schemes and physical equipment or modules.
The result is fresh two-human non-test calibration agreement; it is not
frozen-test formal IAA and does not establish uniformly reliable agreement
across all four entity types.
