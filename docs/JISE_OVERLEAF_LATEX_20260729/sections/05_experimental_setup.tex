\section{EXPERIMENTAL SETUP}

All final results come from the frozen 170-passage domain test split using
$K=5$ and random seeds 42, 43, and 44. The primary extractor is
\modelname{Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct} \cite{qwen2024qwen25}, deployed locally in
FP16. The evaluated scope is limited to four configurations and three seeds,
yielding 12 completed run units. Each unit has 170 traceable per-query records,
and the observed parse-failure count is zero. Tables report the mean and sample
standard deviation over the three seeds.

The final configurations are NER-only: relation types are empty and no
relation-extraction metric is reported. Zero relation predictions in this
configuration are not relation-extraction results. The final test package does
not contain E or E-prime verifier configurations, public-benchmark evaluations,
$K=1$ or $K=10$ scans, or additional seeds. With only three seeds and no
hypothesis test, all between-configuration readings in this paper are
descriptive rather than statistically significant findings.
