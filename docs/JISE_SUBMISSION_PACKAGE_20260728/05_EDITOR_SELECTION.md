# JISE Associate Editor Selection

JISE's official submission policy requires authors to choose **two** Associate
Editors whose research interests are most relevant. The journal warns that a
paper can be rejected without review if it falls outside the selected editors'
areas or reviewers cannot be found.

list on 2026-07-28:

## Mandatory Author Conflict Check

For each author and each proposed editor, check at minimum:

- current institutional affiliation or a planned move to the same institution;
- coauthorship, grant, project, or close collaboration in the journal's
  applicable recent-conflict window;
- current or former supervisor/advisee relationship;
- family, close personal, financial, or adversarial relationship;
- any other circumstance that could reasonably affect impartiality.
